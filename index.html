The problem

Kerala has talent.
What it lacks is access.

We are the most educated state in India, and still our young people wait years for a job. Three enemies keep us out. We're going after all three.

01

Nobody told you

Most openings go to referrals or never reach a job site. You can't apply for a job you never heard about.

We fight back withDomain groups and instant alerts. Leads post openings straight to you, including the hidden ones.
02

You can't crack it

CV ignored. Interview frozen. Nobody showed you what recruiters look for or how to answer.

We fight back withMock interviews, CV teardowns and answer guides from people who actually hire.
03

The skill gap

College taught one thing. Jobs ask for another. That gap is where years of our lives go missing.

We fight back withSkill roadmaps per domain, study circles and free resources that close the gap fast.
Our pledge

Six promises.
No exceptions.

This is what every member gets, and what we'll never compromise on.

01

Every opening, shared

If a member hears of a job, the group hears of it. No gatekeeping.

02

Never pay for a job

Koottam is free. Anyone asking you for money for a job or visa is a scam, and we'll call it out.

03

Referrals from inside

Industry leads in every group open doors that job portals can't.

04

Prep for everyone

Mock interviews, CV reviews and English practice, not just for the lucky few.

05

Skills, for free

Clear roadmaps per domain so you know exactly what to learn next.

06

Nobody left behind

Freshers, career-breakers, Gulf returnees, the employed and the unemployed. All of us.
