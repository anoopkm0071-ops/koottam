Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

In short: we only ask for what we need to put you in the right group. We never sell your data, and you can leave and have it deleted at any time.

What we collect

  • When you join: your name, WhatsApp number, education, the domain you want a job in, whether you're employed, the group we put you in, and the member number of whoever invited you (if anyone).
  • When you post a job, share a story or report a scam: what you type into that form.
  • We don't use advertising trackers. If we turn on visitor statistics, we use a privacy-friendly service that doesn't track you across sites.

Why we collect it

  • To add you to the right WhatsApp group and send you your invite.
  • To share job openings, referrals and prep that match your domain.
  • To show public totals on the website, such as how many people have joined and from which domain. These totals never include names or numbers.

Who can see it

  • Your details are stored in a private Google Sheet that only the Koottam team can open.
  • Inside a WhatsApp group, other members can see your name and number. That's how WhatsApp groups work, so only join if you're comfortable with that.
  • Success stories show only your first name and the story you chose to share.
  • We never sell your data or share it with advertisers. We'd only pass it on if the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while you're a member. When you leave and ask us to delete them, we remove them from our sheet.

Your choices

  • Leave anytime: exit the WhatsApp group, or message a group admin and say "remove me".
  • Delete your data: message a group admin and say "delete my data". We'll remove your details and confirm.
  • Correct something: message a group admin with the change.

Age

Koottam is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page and tell members in the groups.
