Scam alert

If they ask for money,
it's a scam.

Fake agents target job seekers, especially for Gulf and abroad jobs. Report them here and we'll warn every group.

Red flags

  • They ask for a fee, deposit or "processing charge" for a job or visa
  • An offer without any interview
  • Only a personal WhatsApp number, no office or website
  • Pressure to pay today or "lose the seat"
  • "Guaranteed" visa or salary promises that sound too good
  • They want your original passport or certificates

Going abroad? Check the agent is registered on eMigrate (Government of India) before you hand over anything.
