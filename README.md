# Koottam ✦ A job for every Mallu

A movement site for Kerala job seekers. People join through a 5-question guide (name, WhatsApp number, education, domain, employed or unemployed), land in the right group, and get a shareable member card with a personal invite link. The site also has free interview prep, a CV template, skill roadmaps, a hiring form, success stories and scam reporting.

- `index.html` – the whole site (no build step)
- `apps-script/Code.gs` – Google Sheet backend for sign-ups and live counts
- `privacy.html` – privacy policy
- `og.png`, `favicon.svg`, `icon-180.png` – share preview image and icons
- `SETUP.md` – how to go live (GitHub Pages, sheet, WhatsApp links, founder section, analytics)
